Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

We want you to feel confident ordering with us. These guidelines explain how we use your information and what you can expect from Chuks Kitchen.

Privacy Policy

We collect the information needed to create your account, process orders, deliver meals, and provide customer support. This may include your name, contact details, delivery address, and order history.

  • We use your details to fulfill and communicate about your orders.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

  • You may contact us to update your details or ask questions about your information.

By using Chuks Kitchen, you agree to provide accurate account information and use the service lawfully. Please review your order details carefully before submitting an order.

  • You are responsible for keeping your account details private.
  • We may contact you when an order needs clarification or an update.

These policies are provided for this project and may be updated as the service grows. Last updated: September 2026.
